Title: Knowledge-Enhanced Agentic Vulnerability Repair

TL;DR: Agentic AVR grounding patch generation in verified program facts and historical vulnerability knowledge; ReAct-style tool-augmented reasoning; 83.64% repair rate on C/C++ (46/55) and 85.55% on multi-language (148/173); fixes 9 unique CVEs that PatchAgent cannot

Key Result: Knowledge-Enhanced AVR: view-specific vulnerability knowledge + verified program facts + exemplar retrieval; 83.64% repair rate; fixes 9 unique bugs SOTA misses
