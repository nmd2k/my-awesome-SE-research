Title: From Failed Trajectories to Reliable LLM Agents: Diagnosing and Repairing Harness Flaws

TL;DR: Trace-guided framework diagnoses agent failures and repairs harnesses via HTIR (Harness-aware Trace IR); maps failures to ETCLOVG layers; improves held-out test performance 15.2%–50.0% over initial harnesses across SWE-Bench, Terminal-Bench, GAIA, AppWorld
