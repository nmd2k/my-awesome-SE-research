Title: Can LLMs Deobfuscate Binary Code? A Systematic Analysis of Large Language Models into Pseudocode Deobfuscation

TL;DR: First benchmark for LLM binary deobfuscation; reasoning > scale for obfuscation robustness

Brief Summary: First comprehensive benchmark for LLM-based binary deobfuscation spanning pre-compilation, compile-time, and post-compilation stages; 9 LLMs evaluated.

Key Result: Reasoning capability > model scale for robustness; task-specific fine-tuning > domain pre-training.
