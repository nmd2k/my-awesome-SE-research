Title: Automated Repair of TEE Partitioning Issues via DSL-Guided and LLM-Assisted Patching

TL;DR: DSL-guided plus LLM patching for TEE partitioning vulnerabilities; 87.6% repair success on PartitioningE-Bench, 2 real PRs merged

Brief Summary: DSL-guided and LLM-assisted patching for bad TEE partitioning issues, including repair-template instantiation and generated test clients.

Key Result: Achieves 87.6% repair success on PartitioningE-Bench and has 2 merged repair PRs in real-world TEE projects.
