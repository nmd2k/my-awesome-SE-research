Title: NeuroLog: Reasoning You Can Audit -- Neuro-Symbolic Vulnerability Discovery via LLM Facts, Datalog, and SMT

TL;DR: Compile-free vulnerability discovery pipeline: LLM-extracted Datalog facts + SMT solver + crash synthesis; rediscovers 8 CVEs (incl. CVSS-9.8 curl heap overflow) and surfaces 5 new memory-safety bugs on libarchive with $0.005 LLM cost per target
