Title: TRACE: Temporal Relationship-Aware Conversational Entrainment Detection in Dyadic Speech

TL;DR: Configurable repository-level benchmark decoupling coding capabilities into atomized fine-grained tasks to dissect cognitive demands; 78.55% validity yield vs 31.7% of SWE-bench Verified.

Brief Summary: Configurable repository-level benchmark decoupling coding capabilities into atomized fine-grained tasks to dissect cognitive demands; 78.55% validity yield vs 31.7% of SWE-bench Verified.

Key Result: Reveals capability misalignment: distinct ranking shifts across cognitive dimensions show coding proficiency is non-monolithic.
