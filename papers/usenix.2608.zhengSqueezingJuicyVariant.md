Title: Squeezing Juicy Variant Bugs Out of Modern Browsers

TL;DR: Vulnerability discovery approach built upon variant analysis, applied on Chromium core upon 6 vulnerabilities pattern, resulting 16 bug reports, 4 assigned CVEs. The methodology is generalize the existed bugs with 4 propertise: Context, Assumption, Violation and Abuse, and then mutating the scanning query for 4 scenarios: Clone code, Same Callee, Same Caller, and Same Callee Chain. The engine to scan is Semgrep.
