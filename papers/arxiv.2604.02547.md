Title: Beyond Resolution Rates: Behavioral Drivers of Coding Agent Success and Failure

TL;DR: 9,374 trajectories: context-gather-before-edit, not trajectory length, predicts coding agent success

Brief Summary: Large-scale behavioral analysis of 9,374 agent trajectories from 19 agents (8 frameworks × 14 LLMs) on 500 SWE-bench tasks; studies outcome-level, failure root-cause, and behavioral patterns.

Key Result: Context-gather-before-edit + validation investment predict success; LLM drives outcome more than scaffold.
