Title: AgentLTL: A Trace-Verification Framework for Measuring, Enforcing, and Training Procedural Compliance in Tool-Using LLM Agents

TL;DR: First-Order LTL-based trace verification for procedural compliance in tool-using agents; block-and-warn harnessing improves compliance; finetuning with compliance reward: +38pp accuracy, +17.5pp compliance

Brief Summary: First-Order Linear Temporal Logic (FO-LTL) derived language for expressing procedural rules over agent traces; yields judge-free deterministic compliance score; two applications: harnessing (gate tool calls online) and finetuning (dense reward).

Key Result: Block-and-warn harnessing improves compliance on 5 of 7 models; finetuning: +38 pp accuracy, +17.5 pp compliance on held-out patterns including unseen tool-name aliases; evidence of learning procedural structure.
