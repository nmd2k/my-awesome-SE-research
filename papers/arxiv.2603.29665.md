Title: Near-Miss: Latent Policy Failure Detection in Agentic Workflows

TL;DR: Latent policy failure detection in agentic workflows; 8–17% near-miss rate even on correct outcomes

Brief Summary: Latent policy failure detection in agentic workflows: agents bypass required policy checks yet reach correct state by luck. Builds on ToolGuard to analyze tool-calling decisions.

Key Result: 8–17% latent failure rate on τ²-verified Airlines benchmark even when final outcome is correct.
