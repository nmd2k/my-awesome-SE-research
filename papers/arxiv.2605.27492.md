Title: Benchmarks are Not Enough: RAMP for Runtime Assessing of Agentic Models in Production Systems

TL;DR: Production-style compiler workflows expose long-horizon agent collapse: 100% initial-stage completion falls to 20% final-stage, with no full-pipeline completions

Brief Summary: Runtime assessment infrastructure for production-style long-horizon software engineering agents, centered on compiler-construction workflows, staged dependencies, partial-failure recovery, and utility metrics.

Key Result: Across 15 models, completion collapses from 100% at the first stage to 20% at the final stage, with no full-pipeline completion and up to three orders of magnitude cost variation.
