Title: Extrapolative Weight Averaging Reveals Correctness-Efficiency Frontiers in Code RL

TL;DR: Studies nested unit-test reward coverage in competitive-programming RL and shows a correctness-efficiency frontier across reasoning, tool-use, and agentic coding settings.

Brief Summary: Studies nested unit-test reward coverage in competitive-programming RL and shows a correctness-efficiency frontier across reasoning, tool-use, and agentic coding settings.

Key Result: Extrapolated checkpoints create complementary policies; ensembles improve pass@250 on LCB/hard by 3.3% at matched sample budget.
