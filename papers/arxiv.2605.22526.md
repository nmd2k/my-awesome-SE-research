Title: "Refactoring Runaway": Understanding and Mitigating Tangled Refactorings in Coding Agents for Issue Resolution

TL;DR: 3,691 Multi-SWE-bench patches: tangled refactorings reduce compilability; refactoring-aware refinement lifts compilability 19.34% -> 38.33%

Brief Summary: Empirical study of tangled refactorings in coding-agent issue resolution using 3,691 valid Multi-SWE-bench patches from 3 agent frameworks and 12 LLMs; proposes refactoring-aware refinement.

Key Result: Agents refactor less often than humans but more diversely; tangled refactoring is linked to lower compilability. Refinement improves compilability 19.34% -> 38.33% and resolves 2.79% more issues.
