Title: Challenges and Future Directions in Agentic Reverse Engineering Systems

TL;DR: Survey and analysis of LLM-based agentic systems for binary reverse engineering (static, dynamic, hybrid agents); identifies limitations including token constraints, struggle with obfuscation, and lack of program guardrails; cutting-edge systems still fail on complex RE with obfuscation and unique architectures.

Brief Summary: Survey and analysis of LLM-based agentic systems for binary reverse engineering (static, dynamic, hybrid agents); identifies limitations including token constraints, struggle with obfuscation, and lack of program guardrails; cutting-edge systems still fail on complex RE with obfuscation and unique architectures.

Key Result: Structured examination of technical capabilities and gaps in agentic RE; roadmap for overcoming token/obfuscation/guardrail challenges.
